## ✨ What's new in v0.1.3

### Window controls
- **Pin button (📌)** — toggles always-on-top *and* locks the window in place so you can't accidentally drag it. Click again to release.
- **Minimize button (−)** — minimizes the widget to the taskbar like a normal app.

### Reliability
- **Polling interval** bumped from 45 s → 90 s. The Anthropic OAuth usage endpoint was returning 429 (rate-limit) too often at the previous cadence; the wider window makes those errors much rarer while still feeling fresh.

### Downloads
- **Linux** — `AI.Quota.Widget_0.1.3_amd64.AppImage` (or `.deb` / `.rpm`), or `Quota-v0.1.3-linux-portable.tar.gz`
- **Windows** — `AI.Quota.Widget_0.1.3_x64-setup.exe` (NSIS) / `AI.Quota.Widget_0.1.3_x64_en-US.msi` (MSI), or `Quota-v0.1.3-windows-portable.zip`
